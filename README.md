FlowPilot AI sits above the tools a delivery organisation already uses (ERP, CRM, HRMS, Jira, collaboration suites) and turns all that scattered data into one clear picture: what's on track, what's slipping and what to do about it.

> **Status:** early-stage startup concept by [Girish S](https://github.com/girishgiris1523-eng). This repo shares the product vision and roadmap.

---

## The problem

Delivery leaders spend hours stitching together status from a dozen systems. By the time a risk shows up in a weekly report, it's already a missed deadline. PMOs are stretched, and executives get opinions rather than evidence.

## The idea

One intelligence layer that connects to existing systems, learns how delivery really flows and gives every level, from Scrum team to C-suite, timely, AI-driven insight.

## Product suite

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Delivery Intelligence** | Live health of every project, sprint and release |
| **PMO** | Portfolio governance, RAID tracking and standardised reporting |
| **Executive AI** | Board-ready dashboards and plain-English answers |
| **Analytics** | Trends in velocity, quality, cost and utilisation |
| **Knowledge AI** | Searchable memory of decisions, lessons and docs |
| **Resource Optimizer** | Smarter allocation of people across projects |

## AI agent stack

- 🧭 **AI Scrum Master**: flags blockers, prepares stand-up summaries and nudges the team
- 📋 **AI Project Manager**: tracks scope, timelines and dependencies
- 🧪 **AI QA Manager**: watches defect trends and release readiness
- ➕ More agents planned for finance, resourcing and risk
